NIDAA FOR ANDROID AND WEAR OS

Your privacy, explained.

A clear look at how Nidaa uses location, camera access and saved settings.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This is the policy for Android and Wear OS. Using an iPhone? See the iPhone and Apple Watch policy.

This policy describes Nidaa’s current data practices. We will update it if those practices change.

Prayer times stay on your device

Nidaa calculates prayer times and Qibla direction on your device. You do not need an account. The app contains no advertising, and Nidaa has no analytics or tracking of its own. We do not operate a server that receives your location, your prayer settings or your camera images. Some Google services that the app uses collect their own diagnostics. This policy describes each of them below.

Location and saved settings

Nidaa stores your saved place (city name, time zone and coordinates) and your prayer preferences on your device. The app, its home screen widgets and its Quick Settings tile read these settings from storage on your device.

Location permission is optional. Nidaa first explains why it needs your location, and then Android asks for permission. The app uses your location only while you use it and never asks for background location. You can choose a city or enter coordinates instead.

If you allow precise location, Nidaa uses your exact position. If you allow approximate location, the app works the same way: only the distances to nearby mosques are less exact. Your location leaves your device only to look up the name of your place and to find nearby mosques, as described below.

Wear OS, widgets and the countdown

If you use Nidaa on a Wear OS watch, your phone sends your saved place (city name, time zone and coordinates rounded to about 100 m) and your prayer-time settings to your watch through the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play services, so the watch can show the same times. While the prayer countdown runs, your phone also sends it to your watch. This data goes only between your own phone and watch, directly or through Google’s servers when they are not connected to each other. Nidaa does not receive it.

The optional prayer countdown appears as an ongoing notification. On Android 16 it can also appear as a Live Update in the status bar and on the lock screen. Home screen widgets, the Quick Settings tile, the countdown and the watch’s tile and complications all run on your devices and use no push service.

City searches and place names

When you search for a city, Nidaa sends your search text to Android’s geocoding service. When Nidaa looks up the name of a place from coordinates (your current location or coordinates you entered), it sends those coordinates to that service. On most Android phones, Google provides this service and handles these requests under its privacy policy.

These requests need internet access. Nidaa also suggests and names cities from a list that is included in the app. That list is searched on your device. Entering coordinates manually still works if a lookup is unavailable. Prayer calculations do not use an online prayer-time service.

Nearby mosques

When you open the Mosques tab, Nidaa sends the location it searches from (your current location if you allow it, otherwise your saved place) to Google Maps Platform. Google uses it to show the map (Maps SDK for Android), to find nearby mosques and prayer rooms and their details (Places SDK for Android), and to estimate walking, driving and public transport times to them (Routes API).

The Maps and Places SDKs also collect their own diagnostics: crash logs, performance data, map interactions such as panning and zooming, and an identifier that Google uses to count the people who use the SDKs. Google handles all of this data under its privacy policy.

Nidaa does not store this information on any server of its own and does not share it with anyone else. If you choose directions in Google Maps, Waze or another maps app, the destination is passed to that app.

Camera and Qibla AR

Camera access is optional. Nidaa asks for it only when you switch the Qibla screen to AR. The camera is used only on your device: Nidaa does not record, save or upload camera images, and no camera image or location leaves your device for AR.

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

ARCore collects its own diagnostics and identifiers: performance data, information about how the app uses ARCore, and a device identifier, or your Google account identifier if you are signed in to Google on your phone. ARCore starts only when you switch to AR. If you never use AR, it collects none of this.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Nidaa schedules prayer alerts and reminders on your device. If you also allow alarms and reminders, Android lets the alerts sound at the exact minute. After your phone restarts, Nidaa schedules them again. You can change sounds and reminders in the app or turn off notification permission in your phone’s settings.

Your choices and data retention

You can revoke location, camera and notification permissions in your phone’s settings and replace the saved place in Nidaa. Settings remain on your device until you change them, clear the app’s data or remove the app. There is no Nidaa account or developer-held copy of your app settings to delete.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include Nidaa’s settings and saved place in your device backup, for example in your Google account. This backup belongs to Android and to you, not to Nidaa: you control it in your phone’s settings.

If you contact support

If you email nidaaprayer@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. If you use Send Feedback in the app, the draft email also includes the app version and build number, Android version and app language, which you can edit or remove before sending. We use it to respond to your request. Your email provider and ours process the correspondence as part of delivering and storing email.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or where retention is required by law. You may ask us to delete it by emailing the same address.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or contact forms. The site remembers the language you choose in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact nidaaprayer@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will publish the updated version here with a new effective date.
